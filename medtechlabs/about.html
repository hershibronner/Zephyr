Our story

It doesn't have to work this way.

Most lab companies started in a boardroom. Ours started with a simple frustration: facilities waiting days for results that should take hours, calling support lines that never picked up, working with labs that treated them like account numbers.

We thought: it doesn't have to work this way.

So we built MedTech Labs — a lab that keeps its promises and treats every facility like our only facility. Today we serve 100+ buildings across 9 states, and we run the same way we did on day one: fast, personal, accountable.

What we believe

01

Speed is a form of care

A result that arrives late might as well not arrive. Behind every “pending” status is a resident waiting for treatment and a family waiting for answers. That's why 24 hours isn't our goal — it's our standard.

02

Every sample is a person

A parent. A grandparent. Someone's whole world. We never forget what's actually inside those vials — and it shows in how carefully we handle every single one.

03

Partners, not vendors

Vendors send invoices and disappear. Partners solve problems and show up. We know which one we are. Ask any of our facilities.
