# Zephyr — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2 August 2026

Zephyr is a fitness and nutrition tracking app. This policy explains what it collects, why, and
where it goes.

## The short version

**Your data stays on your phone.** Zephyr has no account system, no server, and no analytics. It
does not transmit your weight, your food log, your workouts, your location or your step counts
anywhere. There is nothing to breach because there is nothing stored elsewhere.

## What Zephyr stores on your device

All of the following is written to a database in the app's private storage and never leaves your
phone except through a backup or export that you initiate:

- **Profile:** sex, birth year, height, weight, goal weight, activity level and goal pace. Zephyr
  asks for a birth *year* rather than a full date, because the calorie equation only uses age in
  years.
- **Food log:** what you logged, when, and its nutrition.
- **Body weight:** the entries you record and the smoothed trend derived from them.
- **Activity:** runs, hikes, walks and strength sessions, including the GPS route of any session you
  choose to track.
- **Steps:** daily and hourly step counts read from your phone's hardware step counter.
- **Settings:** your training schedule, notification preferences and quiet hours.

## Permissions and why they're needed

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| **Location** (while in use) | To measure distance, pace and elevation during a run or hike you start. Only collected while a session is actively running, and only if you start one. Zephyr does **not** request background location. |
| **Physical activity** | To read the step count from your phone's hardware step counter. |
| **Camera** | To scan a food barcode. Images are processed on-device and never stored or transmitted. |
| **Notifications** | To deliver the reminders and nudges you configure. |
| **Internet** | Only to look up food products, described below. |

You can decline any of these. Zephyr degrades gracefully: refuse location and you can still enter
runs manually; refuse the camera and you can still search or type foods.

## The one time data leaves your phone

When you **search for a food by name or scan a barcode**, Zephyr sends that search term or barcode
to [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free, open food database, in order to look
up nutrition information. Their privacy policy applies to that request.

What is sent: the search text or the barcode number, and an identifier naming the app and version.

What is **not** sent: your identity, your food log, your weight, your location, or any other
personal data. The lookup is not linked to you. Results are cached on your phone so the same product
doesn't need to be looked up again.

If you never search or scan, Zephyr makes no network requests at all.

## Analytics and advertising

There are none. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifiers, no
tracking of any kind.

## Backups

If you have Android's cloud backup enabled, your Zephyr data may be included in your device backup,
which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. You can disable this in your device's
backup settings.

## Sharing

Zephyr does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone. There is no third party to share it
with.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Zephyr deletes everything it stored. You can also clear the app's data from Android
Settings → Apps → Zephyr → Storage. There is no server-side copy to request deletion of.

## Children

Zephyr is not directed at children under 13 and should not be used by them. Calorie restriction is
not appropriate for children.

## Health disclaimer

Zephyr provides general fitness and nutrition information. It is **not medical advice**, and it is
not a medical device. It will not knowingly prescribe an unsafe calorie target — deficits are capped
and floored — but it cannot account for your medical history. If you have a health condition, are
pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, speak to a qualified professional before changing
how you eat or train.
